# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: October 4, 2026** This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with the Bot. ## 1. Information We Collect The Bot may process the following information when necessary for its functionality: - Discord User ID - Discord Server ID - Discord username and display name - Information provided by users when interacting with the Bot - Product and order information - Order status and transaction-related information - Information required to provide administrative and customer support features The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information that is not necessary for its functionality. ## 2. How We Use Information Information processed by the Bot may be used to: - Process and manage customer orders - Provide products and services requested by users - Send order status notifications - Identify users and associate orders with the correct Discord account - Provide customer support - Provide administrative features for authorized server administrators - Prevent abuse and maintain the security and reliability of the Bot - Improve the functionality and reliability of the service We only process information that is reasonably necessary for these purposes. ## 3. Discord Data The Bot operates through the Discord platform and may receive information through Discord's API and Gateway events. The Bot does not use Discord data for spam, unsolicited advertising, or purposes unrelated to the Bot's functionality. Discord data is handled in accordance with Discord's applicable policies and terms. ## 4. Order and Transaction Information When a user creates an order, the Bot may store information such as: - Discord User ID - Order ID - Purchased product - Quantity - Order amount - Order status - Date and time of the order - Transaction verification information provided for processing the order Payment information such as bank account passwords, card passwords, or authentication credentials is not requested or intentionally stored by the Bot. ## 5. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or trade user information. Information may only be shared when necessary to: - Provide the requested service - Process or verify an order - Provide customer support - Protect the security of the service - Comply with applicable legal requirements We do not use user information for targeted advertising. ## 6. Data Retention We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's functionality, maintain order records, resolve disputes, provide support, and comply with applicable obligations. When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized. ## 7. Data Deletion Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their Discord account. To request deletion, please contact the Bot administrator through the official support channel or contact method provided by the Bot. When a valid deletion request is received, we will review the request and delete applicable information where reasonably possible, except where retention is necessary for legitimate purposes or required by law. ## 8. Data Security We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the Bot against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. ## 9. Children's Privacy The Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and the Bot. ## 10. Third-Party Services The Bot may rely on third-party services such as Discord and infrastructure or hosting providers to operate its functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. ## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically. ## 12. Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, data processing, or a data deletion request, please contact the Bot administrator through the official support server or contact method associated with the Bot. --- **By using the Bot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.**
